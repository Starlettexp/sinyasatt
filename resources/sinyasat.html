Platform Charter About Sinyasat · Vision, Mission & Core Team Overview

An informed Filipino. A stronger Philippines.

Sinyasat is a digital electoral information platform designed to enhance the political literacy of Filipino voters by providing accessible, organized, and nonpartisan information on candidate track records and electoral procedures.

01 / Our Vision

A democracy shaped by informed choices.

A democracy shaped by informed choices where every Filipino voter has the confidence and knowledge to critically examine candidate track records, question those in power, and recognize their influence as registered voters.

02 / Our Mission

Accessible civic and electoral literacy.

Enhance political literacy by consolidating accessible, nonpartisan candidate backgrounds, legislative track records, voter rights, and COMELEC procedures into a single digital platform—with unverified or missing entries transparently marked.

03 / Core Team

Informed individuals & first-time voters.

Our team—composed of informed individuals and first-time voters—designed a reliable electoral information website that aims to tackle the persistence of uninformed decision-making and biased political resources.

04 / Platform Purpose

Built for first-time and experienced voters.

Built for both first-time and experienced voters to help them evaluate candidate qualifications, legislative projects, and legal records neutrally before making electoral decisions—encouraging every citizen to approach elections with greater awareness.
